Gujarati speech samples

Listen to real recordings next to synthesized versions of the same sentences, and see how closely each system's pitch, loudness, length and voice follow the real speech.

Please read. These are research samples from a work in progress, not a product demo. Fifteen held-out sentences, one real reading each, no listening test. The measurements describe similarity to that one reading; they are not quality scores. The trained pitch and energy controls studied in this project did not work, while a plain signal-processing edit of the output, and a guided sampler that needs no trained control, did (see the Control requests tab).
